An ongoing mobile operations solution for Artha Palma that streamlines digital weighing and farmer transactions. Built around a local-first workflow, it supports NFC farmer card scanning, reliable on-device data capture, delayed synchronization, and clear transaction summaries and receipts, helping field teams keep operations moving even with limited connectivity.
